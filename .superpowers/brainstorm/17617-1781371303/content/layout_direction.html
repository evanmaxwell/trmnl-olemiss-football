How should we increase information density on TRMNL X?

Select the option that best fits your preference for TRMNL X's larger screen area (1040x780 vs 800x480 on OG).

A

Three-Column Dashboard

Keep the left "Up Next" column and the middle "Upcoming Games" column, but add a third column on the right showing the last 3-4 games (results/scores) or SEC standings.

B

Matchup Stats Comparison

Keep the 2-column layout, but expand the "Up Next" hero column to show a side-by-side comparison of key team stats (Ole Miss vs Opponent) like rank, record, points/game, and yards/game.

C

Full-Season Schedule Grid

Use the extra height and width to render a 2-column or 3-column schedule grid on the right, displaying all 12 games of the season at once instead of truncating at 8 games.
